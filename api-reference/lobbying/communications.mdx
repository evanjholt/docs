---
title: "Communications"
api: "GET /api/v1/lobbying/communications"
description: "Retrieve communication reports filed by registered lobbyists."
---

Access records of monthly communication reports describing interactions between designated public office holders and registered lobbyists.

### Query Parameters

<ParamField query="client" type="string">
  Partial match on client organization name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="dpoh" type="string">
  Partial match on the designated public office holder name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="institution" type="string">
  Partial match on the department or institution contacted.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="lobbyist" type="string">
  Partial match on the lobbyist name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="subject" type="string">
  Partial match on the subject labels or subject text.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="date_from" type="string">
  ISO 8601 start date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="date_to" type="string">
  ISO 8601 end date.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="page" type="integer">
  Page number. Default: `1`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="per_page" type="integer">
  Results per page. Default: `50`. Max: `200`
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="limit" type="integer">
  Alias for `per_page`
</ParamField>
